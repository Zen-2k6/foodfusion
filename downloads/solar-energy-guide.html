A Beginner Guide to Solar Energy

Solar panels change sunlight into electricity without burning fossil fuels while operating.

How it works

  1. Sunlight reaches photovoltaic cells in a solar panel.
  2. The cells produce direct-current electricity.
  3. An inverter changes it into alternating current for everyday use.

Benefits

  • Renewable source of energy
  • Low operating emissions
  • Can be installed on many buildings

Limitations

  • Output changes with weather and daylight
  • Installation has an initial cost
  • Storage may require batteries
