Garden Herb Pasta

An easy vegetarian pasta with mushrooms, spinach and fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • 250 g pasta
  • 150 g mushrooms
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Fresh herbs and olive oil

Method

  1. Boil the pasta according to the packet instructions.
  2. Saute the mushrooms and garlic in olive oil.
  3. Add the spinach and cook until just wilted.
  4. Toss with the drained pasta and finish with herbs.

Tip: choose seasonal herbs for the freshest flavour.
